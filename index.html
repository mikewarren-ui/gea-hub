Know Before You Go

Session types & what to expect at Dreamforce 2026

Session Types

Listen & Learn

  • Keynotes — Large-format main stage. Marc Benioff + special guests.
  • Breakout Sessions — 45–60 min presentations. Great for product roadmap depth.
  • Theater Sessions — 20-min rapid-fire demos. Great for Agentforce City. First come, first seated.

Interactive

  • Roundtables — Facilitated peer discussion (10–20 people). GEA's Warranty Roundtable is this format. Reserve ahead — limited seats.
  • Hands-On Training — Labs with direct instruction. Good for Chuck and Sean's technical track.
  • SICs — Your dedicated Salesforce product meetings. GEA has 3 confirmed (Field Service, Analytics, Commerce) and 1 submitted pending scheduling (Observability/Digital Wallet).
Sessions & Agenda Builder
  • Agenda Builder is live at salesforce.com/dreamforce/my-agenda
  • Mobile app launches Sept 2 — download before you go
  • First come, first seated for keynotes and breakouts — arrive 15 min early
  • SICs and roundtables are reserved — check your calendar invites

Campus Experiences & Locations

Campground

The main Dreamforce hub at Moscone Center. Agentforce demos, partner pavilions, and the heart of the conference floor.

Learning Labs

Hands-on sessions with direct instruction. Technical tracks for Sam, Chuck, and Sean's team.

Trailblazer Forest

Outdoor networking space. Community meetups and casual connections between sessions.

Community Cove

Community-driven content, user groups, and Trailblazer meetups. Great for peer networking.

Dreampark

Outdoor activations and Agentforce City. GEA's Bodewell/Sunny was featured here at DF25.

Pro Tips for Success

Arrive Early

Sessions fill fast. For keynotes, arrive 30+ minutes early. For SICs, arrive exactly on time — they start promptly.

During the Event

Wear comfortable shoes. Moscone is spread across multiple buildings. Build in travel time between sessions — 15 min minimum.

Bookmark Sessions on Salesforce+

Can't attend live? Most keynotes stream on Salesforce+. Save your must-see sessions in the app ahead of time.

Install the App Before You Go

The Dreamforce mobile app has your schedule, maps, and networking. Download before Sept 15 — it launches Sept 2.

Safety & Security Best Practices

Keep badges visible. Report anything suspicious to event staff. Emergency exits are marked on all venue maps.
